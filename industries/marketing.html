Curriculum, weeks 2 to 4

Built for marketing & creative. Run on your real work.

WEEK 2 · DEPARTMENT SKILL LABS

One Claude Project per client, the TOV engine

The agency splits into account/PM, content production and reporting tracks. The core deliverable of Week 2 is a Claude Project per major client: loaded with their TOV guide, past approved copy, brand rules and terminology. Every draft and variant produced in Week 2 and beyond pulls from that project, so output sounds like the client, not like generic AI.

Claude Project per client, TOV engine

Each client's TOV, approved copy samples, and brand rules uploaded as a Claude Project. Drafts, variants and adaptations come out in the client's voice, the creative director edits thinking, not formatting.

Briefs, proposals & pitch scaffolding

First-draft creative briefs, proposals and pitch outlines assembled from the account history and case-study library, the strategist adds the insight layer, not the document structure.

Reporting that writes the narrative

GA4, ad-platform and analytics exports go in; a client-ready narrative report comes out in your template. The numbers are pulled straight from the export, Claude's job is explaining what they mean, not calculating them.

Content variant production

One approved concept → platform variants, length adaptations and A/B copy at volume, inside the client TOV project. Human sign-off on everything before it ships.

Skills + Cowork What your teams build, and own

In the labs, each team builds reusable Claude Skills and Cowork workflows on its own client work. No coding required, a Skill is a plain-language file that captures how the client wants the work done, and Cowork runs the multi-step version on your files:

  • A content-variant Skill that turns one approved concept into platform variants and lengths, inside the client's TOV project
  • Monthly reporting: an analytics export in a Drive folder → narrative report drafted in the client template, run as one Cowork task
  • A proposal Skill that assembles a first-draft proposal from your case-study library for a new lead
  • A meeting-follow-up Skill: notes in → action list, follow-up email and updated project doc, filed to the client folder
WEEK 3 · REAL-WORK BRIEF

The week the skill becomes yours

Each team ships one real client deliverable through the new pipeline, a report, a campaign's variants, a proposal, during a normal week, with production hours logged before and after. The review session tunes TOV projects against the team's quality bar, if a draft sounds like a chatbot instead of the client, that gets fixed in the prompt before it ships again.

WEEK 4 · ROLLOUT & DEMO DAY

Owned, measured, handed over

Each function demos what it built. Named owner, one metric each, production hours per deliverable, report turnaround, billable ratio. Handover pack: client TOV Projects, prompt library, Skills and Cowork workflows, plus a page on how to sell the 'we run on AI' story to your own clients.
