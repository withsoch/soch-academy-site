Four modules

From installed to owned.

The AI Training is Module 1 on its own. The Capability Sprint runs all four, one module per week. Click a module to expand it.

MODULE 1

Foundation & AI Training

Week 1 · on-site in Tallinn

+

Week 0 (remote, ~20 min per department, included): a short audit of how each team works, turned into your Claude tooling map, so the on-site day below is pure setup, not discovery.

  • Fundamentals of AI what it does well, where it fails, and how to work with it safely
  • Personal productivity apps set up per person on their real work
  • Prompt engineering getting reliable, repeatable outputs
  • Setup the recommended tools configured per person: Claude.ai Team, Projects loaded with your context, and Cowork where it fits
  • Demo & Q&A a live run on your firm's real documents, every "would it work for" answered hands-on

What people use it for from day one:

Meeting notesPaste a transcript, get action items, a decision log and a follow-up draft in 30 seconds
Email triagePaste an inbox, Claude categorises urgency and drafts replies in your tone
Document Q&AAsk a question of a contract or bundle, Claude reads it and answers with references
Claude ProjectsUpload your templates, SOPs and tone guide, every conversation now knows your firm
Claude CoworkHand Claude a folder and a task, it reads, drafts and edits the files while you do other work
First-draft writingClient updates, proposals and reports drafted from your notes, you edit

MODULE 3

Real-Work Brief

Week 3 · async + review session

+
  • Each department runs a brief pulled from its actual workload, not a textbook exercise
  • Run on real files, during the normal work week
  • Reviewed together with Soch: what worked, what broke, what to build next

MODULE 4

Rollout & Demo Day

Week 4 · showcase + handover

+
  • Each department demos what was built, its Project, Skills and Cowork workflows, to the whole firm

  • Handover pack: every prompt, Claude Project, Skill and Cowork workflow, documented and owned in-house
Connectors & integrations

Wired into the tools you already run on.

A workflow is only useful if it runs on live data. Part of the Sprint is connecting Claude to your real stack through MCP connectors and native integrations, so the systems we build act on your actual files, records and messages, not copies.

Google Workspace

Drive, Docs, Sheets, Gmail and Calendar, so Cowork reads and writes the files and threads your team already lives in.

Comms & project tools

Slack, and the trackers and docs your firm coordinates in, connected so workflows trigger and report where the work happens.

CRM, data & systems

Your CRM, databases and internal systems, connected through MCP so Claude works from the record, not a paste-in.

Custom MCP connectors

Where a tool has no off-the-shelf integration, Soch builds a custom MCP connector, so even bespoke systems are in reach.

Everything runs inside your firm's own Claude and Google Workspace accounts. Soch never takes copies of your files.
