Curriculum

Four modules. From installed to owned.

The Workshop is Module 1 on its own. The Capability Sprint runs all four, one module per week, taking every department from Claude user to Claude builder, with Soch building the working systems your team keeps.

Book a scoping call See pricing
MODULE 1

Foundation Day

Week 1 · hybrid setup · 3.5 hours

+

Week 0 (remote, ~20 min per department, included): a short audit of how each team works, turned into your Claude tooling map, so the setup day below is pure setup, not discovery.

  • Tooling map (30 min): we walk the audit findings and confirm which Claude tool each person gets, and why
  • Setup (90 min): the recommended tools set up per person: Claude.ai Team accounts, Claude Projects configured with your firm's templates and context, and Cowork connected to files for those who need it
  • Demo (30 min): live run on your firm's real documents: chat, a Project, and a Cowork task delegated end to end
  • Q&A (30 min): every "would it work for…?" answered hands-on
  • Wrap (30 min): next steps per person, and what to build first as a firm

What people use it for on day one:

Meeting notesPaste transcript, get action items, decision log, follow-up draft in 30 seconds
Email triagePaste inbox, Claude categorises urgency and drafts replies in your tone
Document Q&A"What does our engagement letter say about liability?" Claude reads the doc and answers
Claude ProjectsUpload your templates, SOPs, tone guide, every conversation now knows your firm
Claude CoworkHand Claude a folder and a task, it reads, drafts and edits the files while you do something else
First-draft writingClient updates, proposals, reports: Claude drafts from your notes, you edit
MODULE 2

Department Skill Labs

Week 2 · remote · function-specific tracks

+

The firm splits into function tracks. Each track learns to use Claude well, prompting precisely and getting reliable outputs, then Soch builds its capability inside the Claude ecosystem: a Claude Project loaded with the right firm context, reusable Skills that capture the team's output formats, tone and templates, and Cowork workflows that run the repetitive multi-step jobs. Everything built is theirs to keep and extend. See the industry curricula for how the labs run in your field.

MODULE 3

Real-Work Brief

Week 3 · async + review session

+
  • Each department takes home a brief pulled from its actual workload, not a textbook exercise
  • Completed independently, on real files, during the normal work week
  • Reviewed together with Soch: what worked, what broke, what to build next
  • This is the week the skill stops being ours and starts being yours
MODULE 4

Rollout & Demo Day

Week 4 · showcase + handover

+
  • Each department demos what it built, its Project, Skills and Cowork workflows, to the whole firm
  • Every workflow gets a named internal owner: accountability, not enthusiasm
  • Each department sets one simple success metric to track from month one
  • Handover pack: every prompt, Claude Project, Skill and Cowork workflow, documented and owned in-house
