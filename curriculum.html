Curriculum

The agenda, hour by hour.

The AI Fluency Day is Day 1 on its own, six hours from foundations to set up on your real work. The AI Capability Intensive keeps Day 1, then adds two build days that take your whole organisation from Claude user to Claude builder, with Soch coaching your team to build the working systems it keeps. We train and enable, your team builds and owns. Both are private to one firm, delivered live online, minimum five people.

Pre-work · Week 0 (online, ~20 min per team, included in both workshops): a short audit of how your teams work, turned into your Claude tooling map, so Day 1 is pure setup, not discovery.

Day 1 · both workshops

DAY 1

Foundations, the AI Fluency Day

On-site · 6 hours · this is the whole 1-day workshop

+
  • Hour 1 · AI foundations: what Claude can and can't do today, where it fits your work, and safe, responsible use under your firm's policy
  • Hour 2 · Prompting that works: context and instruction patterns for reliable, high-quality output, practised on your own documents
  • Hour 3 · Claude Projects: you set up your own, with your firm's templates, SOPs and tone, so every conversation already knows your organisation
  • Hour 4 · Claude Cowork: hand Claude a folder and a task and it reads, drafts and edits your real files end to end while you do something else
  • Hour 5 · Your real work: each person applies it to their actual daily tasks, set up on their own device, with Soch at their shoulder
  • Hour 6 · Live demo, Q&A and what to build first: a run on your firm's real work, every "would it work for…?" answered, and a plan for the next morning

What people use it for from day one:

Meeting notesPaste transcript, get action items, decision log, follow-up draft in 30 seconds
Email triagePaste inbox, Claude categorises urgency and drafts replies in your tone
Document Q&A"What does our engagement letter say about liability?" Claude reads the doc and answers

Days 2 & 3 · AI Capability Intensive only

DAY 2

Build Labs

Live online · 6 hours · organisation-wide

+

Working together as one organisation, your team picks the highest-value, most repetitive work across the business, then Soch coaches everyone to build real capability inside the Claude ecosystem, hands-on over live video. By the end of the day your team has built, itself:

  • Claude Projects loaded with your organisation's context, templates and knowledge base
  • Reusable Skills that capture your organisation's output formats, tone and standard operating procedures
  • The first Cowork workflows that run the repetitive, multi-step jobs on the team's real files
  • Connectors and MCP the team connects to the tools your firm already runs, Google Workspace, Slack, your CRM and more

Soch coaches; your people do the building, so everything is theirs to keep and extend.

DAY 3

Build & Demo

On-site · 6 hours · finish, hand over, own

+
  • Each team finishes and hardens its own workflows on real work, with edge cases handled, Soch coaching from the side
  • Teams add multi-step and agentic automation themselves where the work genuinely calls for it
  • Demo hour: your team demos what it built, its Projects, Skills and Cowork workflows, to the whole firm
  • Every workflow gets a named internal owner, accountability, not enthusiasm, and one simple success metric to track from month one
  • Handover pack: every prompt, Claude Project, Skill and Cowork workflow documented and owned in-house, plus your AI use policy
