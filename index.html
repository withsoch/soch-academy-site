Why now

A chat tab is not firm capability.

Everyone on your team has typed into a chat box. Almost no firm has moved past it — to Claude that knows their context (Projects), does multi-step work on their files while they do something else (Cowork), and carries the firm's own way of working in reusable Skills. That gap is the opportunity — and the firms that close it first get the throughput edge over everyone still prompting one tab at a time.

The Claude ecosystem

The right Claude tool for each job.

Claude isn't one thing. Part of the audit is deciding which of these each person actually needs — most firms end up with a mix.

Claude chat

What it is: ask, draft, summarise and research in the moment, in a conversation.

Best for: everyone, day one — turning notes into a letter, triaging an inbox, synthesising a few sources.

Claude Projects

What it is: Claude that permanently knows your context — templates, tone, SOPs, past work.

Best for: anyone who does the same kind of work repeatedly — a client's tone of voice, a firm's drafting style.

Claude Cowork

What it is: delegate multi-step work on a folder of files — Claude runs it while you do something else, on a schedule if needed.

Best for: repetitive, multi-file jobs — processing a folder of invoices, drafting the monthly report from raw exports.

Claude Code

What it is: build internal tools, connect systems and package firm-wide Skills — the technical end of the ecosystem.

Best for: your ops or technical lead and power users — a small internal tool, a data pipeline, reusable Skills at scale.

Honest answer

Where the human stays in charge — always.

Every firm asks this before they commit. These aren't caveats — they're the constraints that shape how we design every workflow.

Claude gets things wrong — confidently

Hallucination is documented, consistent, and not going away. Claude fabricates case citations, states incorrect figures, and produces plausible-sounding errors with no uncertainty. Every workflow we build includes a human verification step for any factual output. This is a design constraint, not a footnote.

Judgment stays with the professional

Client strategy, ethical calls, complex negotiations, creative direction — Claude doesn't make these decisions. It writes the draft so the lawyer focuses on the argument. It screens the CVs so the recruiter focuses on the conversation. The professional decides; Claude does the typing.

It only acts on what you connect

Claude works with what you give it — the files in a Cowork folder, a Project's context, a connected app. It won't reach into a system you haven't connected, and it isn't a live feed of your firm. We design each Project, Skill and Cowork workflow around exactly what it can see, so your team is never surprised by a gap.

Large files need staged processing

Claude can handle long documents — but not a 400-page case bundle or a year's worth of inbox in one go. Cowork workflows are built to process in stages: chunk, summarise, escalate. We build this into the workflow so your team doesn't have to think about it.

Common questions

Everything you're wondering about.

How do you decide which Claude tools we need?+
We audit first. Before any setup, we map how each department works — the real daily tasks, the tools, the bottlenecks — and produce a Claude tooling map: who needs chat, who needs Projects, who needs Cowork, and who needs Code. Most firms end up with a mix. You only deploy and train on what the work actually calls for, so nothing is bought or learned for the sake of it.
Is this a course, or do you actually set things up?+
We set things up. After the audit, the tools each person needs — Claude.ai Team, Projects, Cowork, and Code where it fits — are configured on their device and connected to your files during the foundation day. The teaching happens on your live setup, not slides. You end day one with working tools, not homework.
How big does our firm need to be?+
The Setup Day fits firms of 1–50 people; the Capability Sprint fits firms of up to 100 with two or more distinct functions. Each on-site session runs with up to 12 people so setup stays hands-on and per person — larger firms split into multiple sessions or route extra departments through the Sprint's remote labs.
What do we need before you arrive?+
Google Workspace (Drive, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar) and a decision-maker in the room. That's it — we handle installation, configuration and accounts on the day.
Is it fully on-site?+
The foundation day is on-site in Tallinn, led by Soch co-founder Rizwan Mahmood (see who delivers your program). In the Sprint, weeks 2–4 run remotely — live skill labs, an async brief, and a demo day — with Soch's technical team delivering throughout.
What happens if someone misses a session?+
Sprint sessions are structured so departments work in parallel — a missed person catches up inside their department's track, and the week-3 brief is async by design.
What about our client data?+
Everything runs inside your own Google Workspace and your own Claude accounts — Soch doesn't take copies of your files. On Anthropic's paid plans (Team and above), your conversations are not used to train their models. We'll walk your decision-maker through the data-handling setup before anything is connected.
We run on Microsoft 365, not Google Workspace. Can this still work?+
Yes — Claude.ai Team, Projects and Cowork work independently of your file system, and Cowork works on whatever files and folders you point it at, whether they live in Google Workspace, OneDrive/SharePoint, or on the desktop. The setup day takes slightly longer for M365 firms; we'll confirm this on the scoping call.
What does it cost to run after the programme ends?+
Claude.ai Team is ~$25/user/month, billed directly by Anthropic — and Cowork and Skills are included in that plan, so there's no separate automation-tool subscription to buy. That's your only ongoing cost: no Soch retainer, and everything your team built runs without us once the handover is done.
What happens after the program ends?+
Every workflow has a named internal owner and a metric, and your team keeps the full handover pack — prompts, Claude Projects, Skills, Cowork workflows, and the AI use policy. Firms that want to go further scope that as a follow-on engagement.
