How we work

Scoping. Delivery. Deal Lock.

The Claude ecosystem has four very different tools, and training on the wrong one is wasted money. So every engagement runs in three phases: we scope how your firm actually works, deliver hands-on training on the tool each person's work needs, then lock the final scope and price for whatever comes next, agreed once you've seen the value, not before.

The program

Two workshops. Same starting point. Pick your depth.

We run one firm at a time. Every workshop is private to your team, personalised to your real work off a short pre-audit, and needs at least five people from your firm. Both open the same way: a needs audit and your Claude tooling map, then your people learn to set up exactly the tools their work needs, chat, Projects, Cowork or Code, live in the sessions. The Fluency Day is the one-day foundation. The Capability Intensive keeps day one, then spends two more days coaching your whole organisation to build its own AI agents on its real work, and own everything it builds.

A workflow follows steps you write out, like a recipe. An agent is simpler to ask for but smarter: you give Claude the goal, it works out the steps itself, and you stay in control.

Common questions

Everything you're wondering about.

How do you decide which Claude tools we need?+
We audit first. Before any setup, we map how your teams work, the real daily tasks, the tools, the bottlenecks, and produce a Claude tooling map: who needs chat, who needs Projects, who needs Cowork, and who needs Code. Most firms end up with a mix. You only deploy and train on what the work actually calls for, so nothing is bought or learned for the sake of it.
Is this a lecture, or hands-on?+
Hands-on, on live tools. It's training, not us doing the work for you: after the scoping audit, each person sets up the tools they need, Claude.ai Team, Projects, Cowork, and Code where it fits, on their own machine with Soch guiding them live over screen share, and learns on their live setup, not slides. You end day one able to do it yourself, not with homework.
What do we need before you arrive?+
Google Workspace (Drive, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar), a decision-maker on the call, and everyone on a stable internet connection. That's it, we guide each person through installation, configuration and accounts on the day.
Is it online or in person?+
It's fully online, delivered live, not a recording. Both workshops run as live video sessions, six hours a day, led by Soch co-founder Rizwan Mahmood (see who delivers your program). Everyone works hands-on on their own machine while we guide over screen share, and the short needs audit beforehand is online too. Soch's technical team supports the build days of the Capability Intensive alongside Rizwan.
Do we have to run it for the whole firm?+
Each cohort is private to a single firm, with a minimum of five people. Bring one team or the whole company; on the Capability Intensive everyone works together as one organisation, so what gets built comes from your highest-value, shared work, not siloed department by department.
What about our client data?+
Everything runs inside your own Google Workspace and your own Claude accounts, Soch doesn't take copies of your files. On Anthropic's paid plans (Team and above), your conversations are not used to train their models. We'll walk your decision-maker through the data-handling setup before anything is connected.
We run on Microsoft 365, not Google Workspace. Can this still work?+
Yes, Claude.ai Team, Projects and Cowork work independently of your file system, and Cowork works on whatever files and folders you point it at, whether they live in Google Workspace, OneDrive/SharePoint, or on the desktop. Setup takes slightly longer for M365 firms; we'll confirm this on the scoping call.
What does it cost to run after the programme ends?+
Claude.ai Team is ~$25/user/month, billed directly by Anthropic, and Cowork and Skills are included in that plan, so there's no separate automation-tool subscription to buy. That's your only ongoing cost: no Soch retainer, and everything your team built runs without us once the handover is done.
What happens after the program ends?+
Every agent has a named internal owner and a metric, and your team keeps the full handover pack, prompts, Claude Projects, Skills, agents, and the AI use policy. Firms that want to go further scope that as a follow-on engagement.
