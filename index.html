Integrations & connectors

Connects to the tools you already use.

This is what makes it firm capability, not a chat window off to the side. Through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and native connectors, Claude plugs straight into the stack your team already runs, so the work happens where your files, tasks and inbox already live.

GD

Google Drive

Claude reads, drafts and edits inside the documents, sheets and folders your team already works in, no copy-paste, no re-uploading.

GM

Gmail

Triage the inbox, draft replies in your tone and pull the thread that matters, from your real email, not a sandbox.

GC

Google Calendar

Schedule-aware assistants that see your week, prep your meetings and turn notes into follow-ups the moment a call ends.

CU

ClickUp

Tasks created, updated and summarised straight from Claude, so the work your team delegates lands in the system they already track.

n8n

n8n automations

For the multi-step jobs, we wire Claude into n8n so whole workflows run end to end on a trigger or a schedule, not one prompt at a time.

MCP

MCP & custom connectors

The Model Context Protocol is the open standard that lets Claude plug into almost any tool you run. If it has an API, we can connect it.

Common questions

Everything you're wondering about.

How do you decide which Claude tools we need?+
We audit first. Before any setup, we map how each department works, the real daily tasks, the tools, the bottlenecks, and produce a Claude tooling map: who needs chat, who needs Projects, who needs Cowork, and who needs Code. Most firms end up with a mix. You only deploy and train on what the work actually calls for, so nothing is bought or learned for the sake of it.
Is this a course, or do you actually set things up?+
We set things up. After the audit, the tools each person needs, Claude.ai Team, Projects, Cowork, and Code where it fits, are configured on their device and connected to your files during the foundation day. The teaching happens on your live setup, not slides. You end day one with working tools, not homework.
What do we need before you arrive?+
Google Workspace (Drive, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar) and a decision-maker in the room. That's it, we handle installation, configuration and accounts on the day.
Is it on-site, remote, or both?+
It's hybrid. The foundation day is on-site in Tallinn, led by Soch co-founder Rizwan Mahmood (see who delivers your program). In the Sprint, weeks 2 to 4 then run remotely: live skill labs, an async brief, and a demo day, with Soch's technical team delivering throughout.
What happens if someone misses a session?+
Sprint sessions are structured so departments work in parallel, a missed person catches up inside their department's track, and the week-3 brief is async by design.
What about our client data?+
Everything runs inside your own Google Workspace and your own Claude accounts, Soch doesn't take copies of your files. On Anthropic's paid plans (Team and above), your conversations are not used to train their models. We'll walk your decision-maker through the data-handling setup before anything is connected.
We run on Microsoft 365, not Google Workspace. Can this still work?+
Yes, Claude.ai Team, Projects and Cowork work independently of your file system, and Cowork works on whatever files and folders you point it at, whether they live in Google Workspace, OneDrive/SharePoint, or on the desktop. The setup day takes slightly longer for M365 firms; we'll confirm this on the scoping call.
What does it cost to run after the programme ends?+
Claude.ai Team is ~$25/user/month, billed directly by Anthropic, and Cowork and Skills are included in that plan, so there's no separate automation-tool subscription to buy. That's your only ongoing cost: no Soch retainer, and everything your team built runs without us once the handover is done.
What happens after the program ends?+
Every workflow has a named internal owner and a metric, and your team keeps the full handover pack, prompts, Claude Projects, Skills, Cowork workflows, and the AI use policy. Firms that want to go further scope that as a follow-on engagement.
